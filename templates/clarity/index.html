A clear start, before you sit down

New to the studio? Here is what your first visit looks like.

  1. 1

    Before you arrive

    We send a short health form on WhatsApp or email. If you have dental insurance or a TPA card, share it and we will check your cover in advance.

  2. 2

    Getting to know you

    We go through your health history, listen to what is bothering you, and explain the examination and any X-ray we suggest.

  3. 3

    A clear next step

    Your dentist walks you through what they found, sets out the options with written costs, and you decide in your own time.

Questions before your visit

You can also call or WhatsApp us. We are happy to answer anything before you book.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Call, WhatsApp or book online and we will find a time that suits you, usually within the same week.

I haven't seen a dentist in years. Is that okay?

Completely. Many of our patients start that way. We begin with a calm check-up and no judgement, then plan anything needed at your pace.

I'm nervous about treatment. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We allow extra time, explain each step before it happens, and agree a signal for you to pause us at any moment.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Always. You get a written estimate for every option, and we tell you before starting if anything changes.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Any earlier X-rays or reports, a list of medicines you take, and your insurance or TPA card if you have one.

Is there parking at the studio?

Yes, there is parking right in front of the building, and the studio is on the ground floor with step-free access.
